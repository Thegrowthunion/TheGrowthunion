Questions

About the packages

What do the three packages include?
Foundation (₹45,000 a month) gives you a clear plan and one growth channel managed properly. Growth (₹1,05,000 a month) adds more channels, a content engine and a sales system. Scale (₹1,85,000 a month) is the full growth engine, with sales training, hiring support and compliance guidance. See the packages page for the full list.
How do payments work?
Packages are billed monthly through Razorpay, so you can pay by UPI, card or net banking. Prices exclude GST. Ad spend and third-party tool costs are paid by you directly to the platforms.
Is there a minimum commitment?
Yes, three months. Real growth needs time to compound, and we would rather be honest about that than sell you a short contract that cannot deliver.
How long before we see results?
Paid advertising can show results in two to four weeks. SEO usually takes three to six months to build up. Social media communities take six to twelve months. We set honest timelines for each channel at the start, and we never guarantee results.
