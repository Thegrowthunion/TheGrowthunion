What Is an AEO Agency, and Does Your Business Need One in 2026?

Ranking on Google is no longer the whole game. Here's what an AEO agency actually does — and how to tell if your business needs one right now.

Type a business question into ChatGPT or Perplexity today, and you'll notice something: it doesn't send you to a list of blue links. It gives you an answer — usually with two or three sources named directly inside it. If your business isn't one of those named sources, you don't just rank lower. You don't exist in that answer at all.

This is the gap an AEO agency is built to close. And in 2026, almost no business in India has one working for them yet — which is exactly why this is worth understanding now, not in two years.

What AEO Actually Stands For

AEO stands for Answer Engine Optimization — the practice of structuring a business's online presence so that AI systems like ChatGPT, Perplexity, Google AI Overviews, and Gemini can find it, understand it, and cite it directly in the answers they generate for users.

It's a close cousin of SEO, but the target is different. SEO optimizes for a ranking position in a list of search results a human scrolls through. AEO optimizes for something narrower and more valuable — being the specific source an AI system pulls from and names when it answers a question directly, with no scrolling involved at all.

SEO gets you a spot on the page. AEO gets you named in the answer — and increasingly, the answer is all a lot of people ever see.

What an AEO Agency Actually Does, Day to Day

A genuine AEO agency isn't running a mysterious black-box process. The real work is specific and can be described plainly:

1

Answer-First Content Structuring

Rewriting key pages so the direct answer to a likely question appears in the first 40-60 words of a section — not buried three paragraphs down after a long introduction.

2

FAQ and Schema Markup

Adding structured FAQPage and Article schema so AI crawlers can parse a page's content as clean question-answer pairs, rather than having to interpret unstructured prose.

3

Entity Consistency Across the Web

Making sure a business's name, description, and details are identical across its website, Google Business Profile, LinkedIn, and directories — AI systems weight consistency heavily when deciding whether to trust and cite a source.

4

Building an llms.txt File

A simple, emerging standard — a text file at a website's root that tells AI crawlers directly what a business does and which pages are most authoritative, rather than making them guess.

5

Presence Beyond the Website

Because AI models draw heavily from community platforms — Reddit answers, Quora threads, and review sites — genuine AEO work often includes building real, non-promotional presence in those spaces too.

AEO Agency vs. SEO Agency — What's the Real Difference?

SEO AgencyAEO Agency
Optimizes for ranking position in search resultsOptimizes for being cited directly inside an AI-generated answer
Success = Page 1 ranking, more clicksSuccess = being named as a source, sometimes with zero click required
Built around keywords and backlinksBuilt around answer structure, schema, and entity trust
Well understood, heavily competitive in most categoriesNew enough that most businesses in India have no strategy here at all
Measured in Google Search ConsoleMeasured by manually checking what ChatGPT, Perplexity, and Gemini actually say

The honest answer is that these aren't competing services — they're complementary. A business generally still needs solid SEO fundamentals for AEO to work well on top of them. Most legitimate providers, including us, treat AEO as an extension of a strong digital foundation, not a replacement for one.

Does Your Business Actually Need One Right Now?

Not every business needs to prioritize this today. But a simple test helps: open ChatGPT or Perplexity right now and ask it a question a real customer might ask — "who's a good [your service] in [your city]." If your business doesn't appear, and a competitor does, that's a concrete, measurable gap costing you visibility in a channel that's only going to matter more.

A Quick Reality Check

Be skeptical of any agency promising guaranteed AI citations or a specific ranking in ChatGPT — there's no paid placement mechanism the way there is with search ads, and anyone claiming otherwise is overselling. Legitimate AEO work improves the odds of being cited through genuine structure and trust signals. It doesn't buy a guaranteed spot.

Where This Is Headed

India's digital marketing industry is enormous — Delhi NCR alone hosts over 50,000 agencies. Almost all of them are still competing purely on traditional SEO and paid ads. AEO is one of the very few areas right now where a business can genuinely get ahead of the market simply by being early, rather than by outspending everyone else.

This is exactly the kind of groundwork we've been building into our own site — structured FAQ schema, an llms.txt file, and consistent entity information across every platform we're listed on. Not because it's a guaranteed shortcut, but because being early to a genuine shift is usually worth more than being thorough after everyone else already is.
