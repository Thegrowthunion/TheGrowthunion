Home/FAQ

Questions founders always ask us.

Honest, direct answers about how we work, what we deliver and what to expect.

What does The Growth Union do?
We are a strategy-led growth consultancy for MSMEs, founders and first-time entrepreneurs. If you already run a business, we help it grow. If you are planning to start one, we help you shape the idea, set it up, launch it and win your first customers, end to end.
Is the 30-minute growth audit really free?
Yes. It is a structured conversation with the founder where we look at your business, tell you what is working, what is not and what to fix first. There is no pitch at the end.
What do the three packages include?
Foundation (₹45,000 a month) gives you a clear plan and one growth channel managed properly. Growth (₹1,05,000 a month) adds more channels, a content engine and a sales system. Scale (₹1,85,000 a month) is the full growth engine, with sales training, hiring support and compliance guidance. See the packages page for the full list.
How do payments work?
Packages are billed monthly through Razorpay, so you can pay by UPI, card or net banking. Prices exclude GST. Ad spend and third-party tool costs are paid by you directly to the platforms.
Is there a minimum commitment?
Yes, three months. Real growth needs time to compound, and we would rather be honest about that than sell you a short contract that cannot deliver.
How long before we see results?
Paid advertising can show results in two to four weeks. SEO usually takes three to six months to build up. Social media communities take six to twelve months. We set honest timelines for each channel at the start, and we never guarantee results.
I do not have a business yet. Can you help?
Yes. Our Launch service takes you from the first idea through validation, business model, registrations, brand, website and launch marketing to your first customers. We scope and price it after a free consultation.
Can you handle legal and hiring along with marketing?
Yes. Legal and compliance work is delivered through our partner advocates and chartered accountants, and hiring support through HR specialists, all coordinated by us so it fits your growth plan.
Where are you based, and do you work outside Delhi NCR?
We have two offices: our head office in Gurgaon and an office at Innov8, Saket, New Delhi. We work with businesses across India and with remote clients worldwide, and most work happens online.
How quickly will you reply?
Within one business day. For anything urgent, WhatsApp is the fastest way to reach us on +91 95186-04324.
