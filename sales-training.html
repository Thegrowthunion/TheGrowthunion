The Methodology

The Emotional Selling Framework

Straight from "Sell Without Selling" — the exact 3-step approach used to close B2B deals without sounding salesy.

1. Feel

Understand the buyer's real, often unspoken concern — before you say a single word about your product.

2. Reassure

Remove risk and objections before they're even voiced — using proof, framing, and the right questions.

3. Elevate

Position your offer as the obvious next step — so closing feels like their decision, not your pitch.
