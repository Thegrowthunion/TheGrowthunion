Google Ads Cost Per Click in India: What to Actually Expect in 2026

The cheapest click in India costs about ₹5. The most expensive crosses ₹150. Here's a realistic breakdown by industry.

"How much does Google Ads cost" is one of the least useful questions to ask in isolation — the honest answer spans a roughly 30x range depending entirely on industry, keyword competitiveness, and targeting. A far more useful question is: what does it cost in your specific category, and what budget actually produces usable data.

Real CPC Ranges by Industry in India (2026)

IndustryTypical CPC Range
Restaurants / Food₹10 – ₹25
E-commerce / D2C₹15 – ₹80
General Retail Shopping Ads~₹24 average
Legal & Real Estate₹60 – ₹120
Healthcare / Fintech₹100 – ₹300+
The 30x spread between industries is exactly why "average CPC" articles are close to useless for actual budgeting — what matters is your specific category, not a national average.

What Budget Actually Produces Usable Results

Below ₹10,000/month, Google's own smart bidding algorithms don't accumulate enough conversion data to optimise effectively — expect to pay more per lead than a properly-funded account, and consider manual CPC bidding with a very narrow keyword group instead. A ₹20,000-30,000/month budget is a more realistic entry point for smart bidding to start working properly, and 30-50 conversions per month is roughly the volume Google's algorithm needs to exit its learning phase.

An Important Cost Most Businesses Miss

The GST Detail That Changes the Math

Google Ads spend attracts 18% GST in India. If your business isn't GST-registered, that 18% is a cost you cannot recover — spending ₹30,000/month on ads means paying roughly ₹5,400/month (₹64,800/year) in GST you can't claim back. This alone is a strong financial reason to register for GST once your turnover approaches the ₹20 lakh (services) or ₹40 lakh (goods) threshold.

How Quickly Results Actually Show

Ads can technically start appearing within hours of launch, but expect 4-6 weeks before bid strategies genuinely stabilise for a new account with no conversion history, and 60-90 days in highly competitive sectors like finance or real estate. Consistency in budget — not stopping and restarting — and regular A/B testing of ad copy meaningfully accelerates this timeline.

How This Compares to Meta Ads

Google Ads captures existing search intent — someone already looking for what you sell. Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook) creates demand through interruption-based discovery. Neither is universally better; read our full comparison of Meta Ads vs Google Ads to decide which fits your specific business first.
