Refund & cancellation policy

Last updated: 9 November 2026

We want billing to be simple and fair. This page explains how payments, cancellation and refunds work for The Growth Union’s services.

1. Billing

Our monthly growth packages are billed in advance, each month, through Razorpay. Prices are in Indian rupees and exclude GST unless stated otherwise. Ad spend and third-party tool costs are not part of our fees and are paid by you directly to the platforms.

2. Minimum commitment

Monthly packages carry a minimum commitment of three months, because real growth work takes time to compound. The minimum term is confirmed in the proposal or on your package page before you pay.

3. Cancellation

After the minimum term you may cancel at any time by emailing hello@thegrowthunion.com at least 15 days before your next billing date. Services continue until the end of the period you have paid for.

4. Refunds

  • If you pay and we have not begun work within 7 days of payment for reasons on our side, we will refund the full amount, less payment-gateway charges.
  • Once work on a billing period has started, fees for that period are non-refundable, because the time and resources are committed.
  • Ad spend, third-party subscriptions and government or registration fees are non-refundable once paid to the platform or authority.
  • Duplicate or failed payments are refunded to the original payment method, usually within 5 to 7 working days.

5. Results

We agree goals and KPIs with you, but results depend on your market, offer and budget, and are never guaranteed. A refund is not available on the basis of results alone.

6. How to reach us

For any billing question, write to hello@thegrowthunion.com or message us on WhatsApp at +91 95186-04324. We reply within one business day.
