Questions

Before you start

I do not have a business yet. Can you help?
Yes. Our Launch service takes you from the first idea through validation, business model, registrations, brand, website and launch marketing to your first customers. We scope and price it after a free consultation.
Can you handle legal and hiring along with marketing?
Yes. Legal and compliance work is delivered through our partner advocates and chartered accountants, and hiring support through HR specialists, all coordinated by us so it fits your growth plan.
Is the 30-minute growth audit really free?
Yes. It is a structured conversation with the founder where we look at your business, tell you what is working, what is not and what to fix first. There is no pitch at the end.
