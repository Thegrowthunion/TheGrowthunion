Our Journey

From Strategist
to Growth Partner

The Growth Union didn't start as a business idea. It started as a frustration — with how businesses were being served by both consultants and agencies. This is how we got here.

Early Career

Policybazaar & The Agency Years

Neeraj begins with 2 years at Policybazaar — building expertise in B2B sales, lead generation, and high-volume acquisition. Then 8 years as a marketing strategist at agencies, managing campaigns across industries and building a deep understanding of what drives real business growth.

2023 — 2024

Independent Consulting

Transitions to independent B2B growth consulting — working directly with founders and CEOs across real estate, hospitality, healthcare, and FMCG on growth strategy and execution.

2023

The Book

Publishes "Sell Without Selling: The Art of Emotional Selling in B2B Sales" — crystallising 10+ years of B2B sales learning into a framework that now powers every TGU engagement.

2025

The Growth Union Founded

TGU is officially launched — a full-stack growth consultancy covering strategy, marketing, sales, digital, legal, and hiring. Offices in Gurgaon, New Delhi, and Panipat.

Building and Scaling
