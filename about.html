Our story

Built by someone who has been on every side of the table.

Neeraj Grover’s career began on a tele-sales floor at Policybazaar, where he rose from sales executive to team lead. He went on to run growth marketing, lead growth at a digital marketing agency (Techmagnate), and head marketing at IClimb, always close to the numbers and to the customer.

He kept seeing the same pattern: consultants who give strategy and vanish, and agencies that execute tasks with no business thinking behind them. MSMEs and founders were stuck between the two. So he founded The Growth Union to close that gap: strategy-led growth, with the hands to carry it out.

Neeraj is also the author of “Sell Without Selling: The Art of Emotional Selling in B2B Sales”.
