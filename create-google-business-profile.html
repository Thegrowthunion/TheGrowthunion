How to Create a Google Business Profile — Step-by-Step (2026)

It's free, takes about 20 minutes done right, and is the single highest-leverage thing a local business can set up. Here's the exact process.

Across nearly every business audit we've done in Delhi NCR, the pattern repeats: a genuinely good business with zero or minimal presence on Google Business Profile. Not because it's difficult — it's free and takes about 20 minutes — but because nobody ever sat down and walked through it properly.

Here's the exact process, plus the specific mistakes that delay verification by weeks.

Step-by-Step: Setting Up Your Profile

1

Go to business.google.com and Sign In

Use a Google account you'll keep long-term — ideally a dedicated business email, not a personal one someone might lose access to later.

2

Enter Your Exact Business Name

Use your real, registered business name only — no keywords stuffed in (like "Best Plumber Gurgaon Sharma Services"). Google penalises this, and it can get a listing suspended entirely.

3

Choose the Right Category

Pick the single most accurate primary category first — this affects which searches you show up for more than almost anything else. Additional categories can be added afterward.

4

Add Your Exact Address (or Service Area)

If customers visit you, enter your precise address. If you travel to customers instead (electrician, cleaning service), set a service area rather than a fixed address.

5

Verify Your Business

Google typically verifies via a postcard mailed to your address (5-14 days), a phone call, or email, depending on your business type and location. Postcard is most common in India.

6

Complete Every Remaining Field

Hours, phone number, website, services list, and a written business description using the actual words customers search for — an incomplete profile is a weaker profile, full stop.

7

Add Real Photos — Minimum 10 to Start

Storefront, interior, products, team at work. Real photos consistently outperform stock images, and profiles with more photos get significantly more customer actions.

The businesses that show up on Google Maps aren't necessarily the best ones. They're the ones that filled out every field instead of stopping halfway.

Mistakes That Delay or Sink Verification

  • Using a virtual office or co-working address without disclosing it clearly — Google can suspend profiles that don't match how the business actually operates
  • Keyword-stuffing the business name — a fast way to trigger a suspension review that can take weeks to resolve
  • Mismatched details between the profile and your website or other directory listings — inconsistency is treated as a trust red flag
  • Losing access to the postcard verification code — if it goes to an old address or gets thrown away, you have to restart the process

What to Do the Same Week It's Verified

Don't Stop at "Set Up"

A verified but static profile still underperforms. In the first week, post your first Google Post (an update, offer, or photo), ask your first few genuinely happy customers for a review, and check that every category and service listed accurately reflects what you actually do. A profile that's active from day one builds momentum faster than one that goes quiet after setup.
