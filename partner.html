The Fine Print — Kept Short

How the Programme Works

Commission
15% of the client's monthly retainer — recurring every month the client stays with TGU, not a one-time payout.
Payout Timing
Paid by the 7th of every month, for the previous month's retainer received from the referred client.
Payout Method
Direct bank transfer. No minimum threshold — you get paid however small or large the commission is.
How Long It Lasts
For as long as the referred client remains an active TGU client. No cap, no expiry date on the commission.
What Counts as a Referral
Any business that signs a TGU mandate and mentions your code, or that you introduce to us directly via this form or a warm intro.
Multiple Referrals
No limit. Every client you bring earns its own 15%, tracked and paid separately, stacking month over month.
Cost to Join
Free. No sign-up fee, no minimum commitment, no exclusivity required.
