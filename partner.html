The fine print, kept short

How the programme works

  • Commission: 15% of the client's monthly retainer, every month the client stays with TGU
  • Payout: by the 7th of each month, for the previous month's retainer received
  • Method: direct bank transfer, with no minimum threshold; payouts are subject to applicable taxes
  • A referral is any business that signs a TGU mandate and mentions your code, or that you introduce to us directly
