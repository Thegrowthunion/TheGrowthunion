Questions

About the growth audit

Is the 30-minute growth audit really free?
Yes. It is a structured conversation with the founder where we look at your business, tell you what is working, what is not and what to fix first. There is no pitch at the end.
What should I prepare?
Nothing formal. Just be ready to share what your business does, roughly how you get customers today, what you have already tried and what feels like your biggest obstacle.
What if The Growth Union isn't the right fit?
We will say so, and point you towards what would suit you better. We would rather give you honest direction than take on a client we cannot serve well.
Can I do it in person?
Yes. Choose an in-person session at our Gurgaon or Saket, New Delhi office on the form, or take it by WhatsApp call or Google Meet.
