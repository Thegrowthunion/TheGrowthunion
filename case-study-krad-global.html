Bringing lead cost down at Krad Global

Krad Global · Export & FMCG

Lead cost down 59% in the first month

A full growth system, built and run alongside the founder's team.

Krad Global wanted leads at a cost that made sense for the business, without adding more tools and agencies to manage.

We built the growth system end to end instead of handing over a plan: positioning, acquisition and tracking working as one.
